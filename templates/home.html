About Customify

Customify is a powerful web application that transforms your Spotify experience by providing advanced music management tools and discovery features. Built with Flask and integrated with Spotify's Web API, our platform offers comprehensive playlist management, music recommendations based on your top track, and full playback control - all through an intuitive, modern interface that enhances how you interact with your music library.

Intelligent Recommendations

Discover recommendations based on your top track from your Spotify account.

Advanced Playlist Tools

Create and manage playlists with bulk operations, smart track addition, and the ability to add multiple tracks to playlists simultaneously.

Track Search & Discovery

Find similar tracks with our "Find Similar Tracks" feature, search the entire Spotify catalog, and explore music that matches your taste.

Full Playback Control

Control your Spotify playback directly from Customify - play, pause, skip, shuffle, repeat, and adjust volume with real-time synchronization.

Top Tracks Analytics

View your most played tracks across last 4 weeks and get recommendations based on your favorites.

Real-time Integration

Seamless Spotify Web API integration with OAuth authentication ensures your data is always up-to-date and secure.
